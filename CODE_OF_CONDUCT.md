# Code of Conduct

We want Jio to be welcoming to contributors of every background and experience
level. This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions, constructive feedback, and different perspectives.
- Respect privacy and consent.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, sharing private
information without permission, and sustained disruptive behavior are not welcome.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [saul@saugar.dev](mailto:saul@saugar.dev).
Include relevant context and links; avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.
Reports will be handled discreetly, with information shared only as needed to
investigate and respond.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently
restrict participation based on the severity and repetition of the behavior.
The same standards apply to maintainers.
